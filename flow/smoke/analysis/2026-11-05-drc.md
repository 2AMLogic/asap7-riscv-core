# DRC violations on the 2026-11-05 counter smoke layout, by rule and cause

`flow/smoke/records/2026-11-05/drc.json` reports **549 violations in seven rule
categories** on the routed placeholder counter. [Decision
0012](../../../spec/decisions/0012-pin-klt-0-9-0-and-ratify-asap7-drc-deck.md)
describes them as "routing-layer track/grid auxiliary rules and two others" and
goes no further. This file classifies every one of the 549 by cause.

**This is layout and DRC only, against a predictive PDK.** ASAP7 is not a
foundry process and this block is never fabricated. Every number below is a
tool result against a predictive model and against a named, pinned rule deck —
it is not a measurement and says nothing about silicon.

**The record is not changed by this analysis.** `flow/smoke/records/2026-11-05/`
is append-only evidence; nothing in it was rewritten, no rule was waived, and
no flow setting was altered. See "What was deliberately not done" at the end.

## Reproduction

The whole report reproduces from the committed GDS and the pinned deck in one
command, in about two seconds. The deck is the one packaged with klt; extract
it from a pinned wheel and check the hash before trusting it:

```bash
# deck must hash to the pin in spec/core.md
sha256sum <site-packages>/klayout_tools/decks/asap7.drc
# 14f146f02cd731de3e0b59452eadfdab9c2c947724a219a1002a31b5e0f12d13

klayout -b -r asap7.drc \
  -rd input=flow/smoke/records/2026-11-05/layout/counter.gds \
  -rd report=/tmp/repro.lyrdb
```

This yields 549 items with exactly the record's per-rule counts: `M4.AUX.2`
204, `M4.AUX.1` 180, `V4.M5.AUX.2` 112, `M5.AUX.1` 20, `M6.AUX.1` 20, `M3.S.2`
7, `SDT.ACTIVE.AUX.2` 6. Every cause below was isolated by re-running the
deck's own predicates — copied verbatim from the deck source — over subsets of
the same layout, so each split sums back to the recorded count.

## M4.AUX.1 — 180 — flow setting

> Table 3.14.2: M4 horizontal edges must be on a 24 nm grid (offset 0)

Deck source: `across_ongrid(m4, :h, 24.nm)` → `m4.ongrid(0.25.nm, 24.nm)`
(`asap7.drc:694`, `:720-721`). It flags a **vertex** whose y is not a multiple
of 24 nm; x is checked only against the database unit, so it always passes.
The deck reads `m4 = input(40, 0)` — drawing datatype only, so the DEF `PINS`
rectangles on 40/251 are not checked, only the routing that lands on them.

The M4 in this layout is 54 merged polygons, every one a rectangle exactly
24 nm tall (minimum width). 45 of them have **both** horizontal edges off the
24 nm grid; 45 × 2 edges × 2 vertices = **180**. They split cleanly in two:

**(a) 104 markers — 26 signal-routing rectangles, off-grid because of the M4
track offset.** Every one has its lower edge at y ≡ 39 (mod 48), i.e. its
centreline at y ≡ 3 (mod 48) — exactly the M4 track grid the request asks for.
A 24 nm-wide wire centred on a track has edges at centre ± 12, so for the edges
to fall on the 24 nm grid the track centre must be at y ≡ 12 (mod 24). The
request sets 3 nm:

```
flow/smoke/run.sh:258
  {"layer":"M4","x_offset_um":0.003,"x_pitch_um":0.048,"y_offset_um":0.003,"y_pitch_um":0.048}
```

An offset of 0 does not help either — it puts the edges at y ≡ 12 (mod 24).
Checked against the deck's own predicate on minimum-width M4 wires centred on a
48 nm track grid: offset 0 → 20 `AUX.1` and 20 `AUX.2` vertices on five wires;
offset 3 nm → the same 20 and 20; offset **12 nm → 0 and 0**.

**(b) 76 markers — 19 of the 28 M4 landing pads of the power-grid via stack.**
These are the 104 × 24 nm M4 rectangles of `VIA45_1_2_58_58`, placed where an
M5 strap meets an M1/M2 follow-pin rail, so their y is a *rail* coordinate, not
a track coordinate. Rails step by the 270 nm row pitch, and 270 is not a
multiple of 24 (270 = 11.25 × 24), so rail y cycles through 0, 6, 12, 18 (mod
24) and a 24 nm-tall pad is on-grid only on the quarter of rails at y ≡ 12.
Measured: VDD rails (1350, 1890, 2430, 2970, 3510) are all off-grid, VSS rails
split 3 off (1080, 2160, 3240) and 3 on (1620, 2700, 3780) — 19 off-grid pads
in total. **No track offset can fix these**; the lever is the PDN recipe (see
"Request fields").

## M4.AUX.2 — 204 — flow setting

> Table 3.14.2: minimum-width M4 must lie on a routing track (2N x 24 nm, offset 0)

Deck source (`asap7.drc:725-729`): take minimum-width M4 rectangles, grow each
by 12 nm top and bottom and move it up 12 nm — a 24 nm rectangle at `[y0,
y0+24]` becomes `[y0, y0+48]` — then check both edges against a 48 nm grid.
A marker therefore appears at `y0` and at `y0+48`, two vertices each, i.e.
**4 markers per offending rectangle**; that is why the record's distinct-y list
contains values 48 nm apart (1068 and 1116, 1191 and 1239, …) that are not
themselves edges of any M4 shape.

51 of the 54 minimum-width M4 rectangles have `y0` off the 48 nm grid;
51 × 4 = **204**. The split is the same two causes:

- **104** — the 26 signal rectangles (`y0` ≡ 39 mod 48). Same cause and same
  fix as `M4.AUX.1` (a): a 12 nm M4 track offset puts `y0` at a multiple of 48.
- **100** — 25 of the 28 PDN via pads. Only the three pads on the y = 2700 rail
  are on the 48 nm grid.

`M4.AUX.2` is the wider net of the two rules. Nine pads pass `M4.AUX.1`
because their edges fall on the 24 nm grid (the VSS rails at y 1620, 2700 and
3780); of those, the six on rails 1620 and 3780 still have their lower edge off
the 48 nm track grid and so are caught here, and only the three on rail 2700
pass both rules.

## V4.M5.AUX.2 — 112 — flow setting (PDN strap width); deck rule read for wide metal

> Table 3.15.1: V4 must be exactly as wide as M5 across the M5 length

Deck source (`asap7.drc:762`): `(vert(v4.overlapping(m5)) - vert(m5))` — V4
vertical edges that do not lie on an M5 vertical edge.

The layout has 56 V4 cuts, all of them in the power grid (`par.json` reports
M5 routing usage 0 %, so no signal net reaches V4). They are the two 24 × 24 nm
cuts of each of the 28 `VIA45_1_2_58_58` placements, dropped into a **120 nm**
wide M5 strap. Neither vertical edge of a 24 nm cut can lie on the edge of a
120 nm strap, so every cut contributes both of its vertical edges:
56 × 2 = **112**. All 56 cuts are flagged — none is exempt.

Isolated with the deck's own predicate on two synthetic cases: a 24 nm
(single-track) M5 wire with one 24 × 24 nm V4 cut on its edges gives **0**; a
120 nm M5 strap with the as-built two-cut array gives **4** (2 per cut). The
rule as transcribed assumes the upper metal is minimum width — the deck says as
much for the V1–V3 family, "the via as wide as the upper metal **across the
track**" (`asap7.drc:639-641`) — and a wide power strap is outside that reading.

The pinned klt agrees and already ships the resolution: its `asap7sc7p5t` PDN
preset sets M5 `width_um` to **0.216** precisely so the tech LEF's wide V4 bar
(`VIARULE M5_M4widePWR0p864`) applies and the cut is exactly as wide as the
strap. This flow does not use that preset (see "Request fields").

## M5.AUX.1 — 20 — flow setting

> Table 3.14.2: M5 vertical edges must be on a 24 nm grid (offset 0)

Deck source: `across_ongrid(m5, :v, 24.nm)` → `m5.ongrid(24.nm, 0.25.nm)`; for
the vertically routed M5 the **x** coordinate is checked.

All M5 is power grid: five straps (VSS at x 1326, 2406, 3486; VDD at 1866,
2946), each merging with its via stack into one rectangle 120 nm wide. Both
vertical edges of each strap are off the 24 nm grid, two vertices apiece:
5 × 2 × 2 = **20**. The record's ten distinct marker x values are exactly the
ten strap edges (1266, 1386, 1806, 1926, 2346, 2466, 2886, 3006, 3426, 3546).

The strap centres follow from the request plus the core origin: x = 1026 +
`offset_um` = 1026 + 300 = 1326, then alternating polarities every half pitch
(540 nm).

```
flow/smoke/run.sh:273
  {"layer":"M5","width_um":0.12,"pitch_um":1.08,"offset_um":0.3}
```

Half the strap width is 60 nm ≡ 12 (mod 24), so the edges are on the grid only
when the centre is at x ≡ 12 (mod 24). Two independent things break that:
the centre (1326 ≡ 6) and the half-pitch step (540 ≡ 12 mod 24), which makes
the two polarities differ by 12 (mod 24) whatever the offset. An exhaustive
sweep confirms **no `offset_um` alone fixes this at `pitch_um` 1.08**; the pitch
must also become a multiple of 48 nm.

## M6.AUX.1 — 20 — flow setting

> Table 3.16.2: M6 horizontal edges must be on a 32 nm grid (offset 0)

Deck source: `across_ongrid(m6, :h, 32.nm)` → `m6.ongrid(0.25.nm, 32.nm)` — for
the horizontally routed M6 the **y** coordinate is checked.

The same shape as M5, one layer up: five M6 straps 288 nm wide (VSS at y 1593,
2673, 3753; VDD at 2133, 3213), each spanning the full core width, two
horizontal edges each, two endpoints each: 5 × 2 × 2 = **20**. The record's two
distinct marker x values are the strap ends (1026 and 3942 — the core edges)
and its ten distinct y values are the strap edges. The M6 `PINS` shapes are the
same rectangles and merge with them.

```
flow/smoke/run.sh:274
  {"layer":"M6","width_um":0.288,"pitch_um":1.08,"offset_um":0.513}
```

Centres are y = 1080 + 513 = 1593, then every 540 nm. Half the width is 144 ≡
16 (mod 32), so edges are on-grid only when the centre is at y ≡ 16 (mod 32).
The half-pitch step 540 ≡ 28 (mod 32) walks the residue by −4 each strap, so
the five straps land on five *different* residues and a single offset can fix
at most one of them. As for M5, the pitch has to change too — to a multiple of
64 nm.

## M3.S.2 — 7 — tool

> Table 3.12.1: min tip-to-side M3 spacing (one edge <= 36 nm, other > 36 nm) 25 nm

Deck source (`asap7.drc:619`): `m3_short.separation(m3_long, 25.nm,
projection)` — a short M3 edge (≤ 36 nm) facing a long one (> 36 nm) closer
than 25 nm.

All seven markers are 24 × 34 nm boxes, and all seven are the same
configuration: the gap between

- a **90 × 34 nm M3 patch** that OpenROAD inserts as `SHAPE DRCFILL` beside each
  power-grid via stack (`counter.def`, e.g. `NEW M3 34 + SHAPE DRCFILL ( 2901
  3510 ) ( 2991 3510 )`), whose 34 nm end is the "short" edge; and
- an 18 nm-wide vertical M3 **signal wire** on the next M3 track, whose side is
  the "long" edge.

Worked example, the first marker: the patch occupies x 1281…1371 at y
2683…2717, the wire occupies x 1395…1413 at y 2524…3416 — a gap of **24 nm**,
one nanometre under the rule. The other six are identical in shape.

The gap is deterministic, not luck. The patch is centred on the strap, so its
right edge is at strap x + 45; M3 tracks are on a 36 nm pitch at offset 0 and
18 nm wires put their left edges at track − 9 ≡ 27 (mod 36). With the strap at
x ≡ 30 (mod 36), the first wire track to the right is always 24 nm away.

This is a tool cause: the patch is the router's own geometry, inserted to
satisfy the router's own area rule, and `par.json` reports
`route_drc_violation_count: 0` because OpenROAD's ASAP7 rule set does not carry
the DRM's length-conditioned tip-to-side spacing. Note also that klt's
`min_area_repair` stage was **skipped** in this run — `"PDK variant 'asap7'
resolves to no curated deck, so no minimum-area rule is available to floor
routed metal against"` — so nothing on the klt side inspected these patches
either.

## SDT.ACTIVE.AUX.2 — 6 — deck + pinned cell library

> Table 3.8.1: SDT (not interacting with SRAMDRC) horizontal edges must coincide with ACTIVE horizontal edges

Deck source (`asap7.drc:408`): `horz(sdt_ns).not_interacting(horz(active))`.
The deck already softens "coincide" to "lies on, and overlaps, an ACTIVE
horizontal edge", because the shipped cells draw SDT 4 nm past ACTIVE's
*vertical* edge and a pointwise reading would flag every conforming cell
(`asap7.drc:403-406`).

**No router geometry is involved.** All six markers are inside the three
`BUFx4_ASAP7_75t_R` instances that clock-tree synthesis inserted, two per
instance, at the same two places in cell coordinates: the horizontal edges at
y = 108 and y = 162, x 42…66.

Checked cell by cell over every master in the GDS, with the deck's predicate
applied to each cell alone: `BUFx4_ASAP7_75t_R` (and its orientation variant)
is the **only** master that trips it. The geometry is the cell's own — its
leftmost SDT finger is drawn x 42…66, y 27…108 (81 nm tall) over an ACTIVE
step that is only x 46…96, y 27…81 (54 nm tall), so the SDT's top edge at
y = 108 has no ACTIVE horizontal edge to lie on. The ACTIVE here is not a
rectangle: its horizontal edges are y = 81 over x 46…96 and y = 108 over
x 96…332.

This is a known, documented deviation of the pinned library, not a new finding
of this flow. klt records it exactly:
`klayout_tools/decks/asap7_rules.py`, `SHIPPED_CELL_DEVIATION_RECORDS` lists
ten cells — `AO33x2`, `BUFx2`, **`BUFx4`**, `BUFx4f`, `CKINVDCx9p33`, four
`ICG*` and `OAI22xp33` — against `SDT.ACTIVE.AUX.2`, citing "Table 3.8.1, Fig.
3.8.1(d)", with the rationale *"SDT drawn 81 nm tall over a 54 nm ACTIVE: the
SDT top edge is not on an ACTIVE horizontal edge"* and the handling *"kept as
stated; expected exactly; left out of the clean run"*. The measurement above
is an independent confirmation of that record on this layout.

The three conditions of the operator ruling in klayout-tools #4530 (violations
inside unmodified foundry cells may be a disclosed exception for T1 item 3) are
met for these six: they reproduce on the bare cell, they are pinned to a named
cell, and they are stated here. **Acting on that is not this issue's job** —
a disclosed exception is a decision record, and #29 owns it.

## Observations recorded, not acted on

- **`spec/core.md` understates the deck's BEOL coverage.** It lists, under "Not
  checked yet", "rules needing track, net, corner or grid predicates (#3147)".
  Five of the seven rules here are grid/track-position rules and the deck
  plainly checks them; `coverage.unchecked_rules` and `coverage.rules_skipped`
  are both empty at 267/267 categories. What the deck actually leaves out is
  narrower — track *assignment* (`M4.W.4`, `M4.S.3-S.5`, `M4.AUX.4` and their
  M5–M7 counterparts, klt #3155), the via-level net comparison, mask colouring
  and PAD. Correcting the spec is a decision record of its own and is out of
  scope here.
- **`min_area_repair` could not see a deck.** `par.json` records it as
  `status: "skipped"`, reason `"PDK variant 'asap7' resolves to no curated
  deck…"`, in the same run where `klt drc --deck-file` checked against one.
  It is adjacent to the `M3.S.2` row but is not its cause, so no issue is filed
  from this pass.
- **The deck reads drawing datatypes only** (`m4 = input(40, 0)`), so the DEF
  `PINS` rectangles written to 40/251 are not checked. The 11 M4 I/O pins are
  covered only through the routing that lands on them.

## What was deliberately not done

- `flow/smoke/records/2026-11-05/` is byte-for-byte unchanged (`git diff
  origin/main -- flow/smoke/records/` is empty).
- No waiver was written. `layout/waivers/` does not exist and was not created;
  waiver policy is [decision
  0005](../../../spec/decisions/0005-drc-lvs-acceptance-and-waivers.md).
- `flow/smoke/run.sh`, the deck pin and `spec/` are unchanged; the request
  fields above are named, not altered.
- Place-and-route was not re-run. Nothing here is a claim that any proposed
  value produces a clean result on this design.
