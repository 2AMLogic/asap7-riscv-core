## Families, each with a measured cause

### PDN M2–M5 via stack — `V4.M4.EN.1` 14,384 + `V4.M5.EN.2` 232

> Table 3.15.1: min enclosure of V4 by M4 (EN.1) / M5 (EN.2) on two opposite
> sides 11 nm

All 14,616 markers are rectangles **exactly 216 nm × 24 nm** — the M5 strap
width (0.216 µm) and the M4 minimum wire height, the same shape the counter
record showed at 28 + 7 markers, scaled by the core's 60 M5 strap columns
against the counter's 5. A 24 nm wire cannot enclose a 216 nm cut row by
11 nm on two opposite sides; no `power.*` request field reaches this
geometry. Upstream fix **merged, unreleased**: klayout-tools PR #7084
("repair the ASAP7 pdngen M2-M5 power stack after the DEF-GDS merge", closes
#6909) takes exactly `V4.M4.EN.1` / `V4.M5.EN.2` / `M3.S.2` to zero; the
newest release v0.9.2 (2026-11-15) predates the merge (2026-11-23), and
this repo's pin bump is tracked by #246 (blocked on the upstream release,
#6930). Waived on that basis, superseded by the first re-routed record.

### Router DRC-fill patches — `M3.S.2` 870

> Table 3.12.1: min tip-to-side M3 spacing (one edge <= 36 nm, other > 36 nm) 25 nm

870 markers of 18 × 34 nm, the router's M3 fill-patch geometry (the
counter's `M3.S.2` family, 7 there). In #7084's zeroing scope with the PDN
family above — its title names `M3.S.2` — so the same waiver and the same
supersession condition apply.

### Router via-pad tips — `M1.S.2` 583, `M1.S.3` 77, `M1.S.4` 1,708, `M1.S.5` 373

> Table 3.12.1: min tip-to-side / tip-to-tip M1 spacings 25–31 nm

2,741 tip markers on top-level router M1 at the protruding pad tips of
router-inserted via stacks — the counter showed 3 (1 `M1.S.4`, 2 `M1.S.5`).
Upstream: #4508 closed via PR #7142 (pad–**pad** repair, merged, unreleased)
and the [2026-11-23 recheck](2026-11-23-upstream-disposition-recheck.md)
measured that the counter's three markers are pad–**wire** pairs outside
its scope, filed as klayout-tools #7397 — closed completed 2026-11-23 with
its fix likewise merged to unreleased main. Waived on that basis.

### Router-emitted M4 off the deck's track grid — `M4.AUX.1` 4 + `M4.AUX.2` 8 + `M4.W.3` 8

> Table 3.14.2: M4 horizontal edges on a 24 nm grid; minimum-width M4 on a
> routing track (2N × 24); Table 3.14.1: vertical M4 width not an even
> integer multiple of 24 nm

Twenty markers, two locations. The 12 `M4.AUX.*` point markers are vertices
of router-emitted **minimum-width (24 nm) M4 routing rectangles whose
horizontal edges sit at y ≡ 12 (mod 24)** — half the grid pitch off every
track centre the request declares (`floorplan.tracks[]` M4 offset 0.012,
pitch 0.048 puts wire edges at ≡ 0 mod 24; measured examples: a wire at
y 53,988–54,012 nm, another at y 54,036). The 8 `M4.W.3` markers are all on
one router M4 escape column 84 nm wide (3.5 × 24 nm) by 0.89 µm tall at
x ≈ 36.23 µm, y ≈ 57.3–58.2 µm, merged with via landing pads (a 5 nm notch
on its right edge at y 57.6 µm). The overwhelming majority of the flow's
M4 is on-grid — these 20 are the only off-grid router emissions — and no
request field reaches them; the router's own DRC reports zero on the same
GDS. Not previously tracked upstream: filed generically as klayout-tools
#7817 (friction protocol; no design-specific detail there). Waived on that
basis.

### Shipped-cell deviation — `SDT.ACTIVE.AUX.2` 2,293

> Table 3.8.1: SDT (not interacting with SRAMDRC) horizontal edges must
> coincide with ACTIVE horizontal edges

**No router geometry is involved.** Every one of the 2,293 markers lies
inside an instance of exactly three masters — `BUFx4_ASAP7_75t_R` (946:
512 N + 434 mirrored `$VAR1`), `BUFx2_ASAP7_75t_R` (692: 354 + 338) and
`OAI22xp33_ASAP7_75t_R` (655: 240 + 415) — verified by a containment walk
of the GDS hierarchy over all 2,293 bboxes (0 markers outside those
instances). All three masters are on klt's shipped-cell deviation list
(`asap7_rules.SHIPPED_CELL_DEVIATION_RECORDS`: "SDT drawn 81 nm tall over a
54 nm ACTIVE: the SDT top edge is not on an ACTIVE horizontal edge"), the
same deviation the 2026-11-05 smoke analysis measured on the bare cell and
the 2026-11-21 record re-measured on the counter's three CTS instances.
The counter's core scale: the core's clock tree and logic place ~480
instances of deviation-list cells, so the count scales from 6 to 2,293.

Per the operator ruling in klayout-tools #4530 (violations inside
unmodified foundry cells may be a **disclosed** exception for T1 item 3),
this family is **stated, not waived**: [decision
0021](../../spec/decisions/0021-sdt-active-aux-2-shipped-cell-disclosure.md)
records the disclosure with the ruling's three conditions verified against
this layout.

## What this means for T1 item 3

Item 3 requires `status: clean` with zero violations against the pinned
deck (spec/core.md "DRC / LVS acceptance"). This record is not clean, so
**item 3 stays `unmet`** — now graded `check_failed` against committed
evidence instead of `no_evidence` (`manifests/asap7-riscv-core.json` cites
this report; the regenerated verdict record shows the citation). The claim
this repo makes is exactly: *not clean; 20,540 markers; every marker
waived with a recorded upstream cause or disclosed by a decision record;
three of four families wait on merged-but-unreleased upstream fixes that
the pin bump #246 tracks.* Deck coverage disclosure, quoted verbatim from
the record's `coverage` block:

- `layers_in_stream_without_rules`: `[]`
- `rules_skipped`: `[]`
- `deck_scope`: `[]` (the deck reports no chapter scoping)
- `unchecked_rules`: `[]`; `unmodeled_rules`: 67 rule ids (listed in the
  record) — the DRM chapters the deck does not transcribe, the known
  decision-0005 unchecked classes (track/net/corner/grid predicates
  #3147, FEOL/MOL increments #2812, PAD) stated by the claim, not hidden.

A clean report needs (a) the klt release carrying #7084 and the #7397 fix
(#246's pin bump), (b) a re-route of the core at that pin (a new par
record and GDS, tracked as #277), and (c) either the disclosed SDT
exception standing (decision 0021) or a CTS/logic cell choice that avoids
deviation-list masters — a flow change, not a deck change. Nothing in this
repo can relax the deck or the spec to make this record pass, and nothing
here does.

## Reproduction

Same one command shape as the smoke analyses, on the committed GDS:

```bash
uvx --from "klayout-tools==0.9.0" klt drc --engine klayout \
  --deck-file <pinned-0.9.0-asap7.drc> --expect-rule-categories 267 \
  --timeout-s 2700 --format json layout/cve2_top.gds
```

yields the committed counts (deck sha256 `14f146f0…e0f12d13`). The
hierarchy attributions above came from a klayout containment walk of the
same GDS (deviation-master placements transformed to top coordinates;
SDT-in-deviation-cells 2,293/2,293, outside 0).
